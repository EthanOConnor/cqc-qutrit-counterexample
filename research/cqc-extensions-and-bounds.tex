\textbf{Status and provenance.}
This note develops consequences of the explicit qutrit-pair counterexample to the complementary-quantum correlation (CQC) conjecture. The original qutrit calculation has been independently reproduced outside the model-assisted workflow. The higher-dimensional computational/Fourier family is algebraic and has been checked directly in the full $d^2$-dimensional space for $2\le d\le 10$. The arbitrary-MUB-pair extension and the conclusions about unrestricted two-qubit states should receive independent scrutiny before being treated as settled theorems.

The analysis and initial verification artifacts were generated by OpenAI GPT-5.6 Pro in a prompted research exchange; Ethan O'Connor is the repository steward. Source, exact qutrit certificate, and verification code:
\href{https://github.com/EthanOConnor/cqc-qutrit-counterexample}{\texttt{github.com/EthanOConnor/cqc-qutrit-counterexample}}.
\end{tcolorbox}
